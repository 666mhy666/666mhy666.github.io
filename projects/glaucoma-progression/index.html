Longitudinal Retinal Thickness in Glaucoma

How does retinal thickness change when patients return at different times? A curved trend fit these data better than a straight line. The selected model also supported stronger within-patient correlation between visits closer together in time.

107 patients · 786 visits · Up to 4.2 years of follow-up · Independent UCLA academic project

View the repository Open the R analysis

The fitted decline became less steep over follow-up

The fitted mean reached a minimum around 3.7 years, then turned slightly upward near the end of follow-up. I treat the small upturn as a feature of the quadratic fit at the boundary and avoid reading it as retinal recovery.

The curve describes the average across patients. In the original patient profiles, starting thickness and rates of change varied within the cohort.

GCC is the retinal ganglion cell complex. Thickness was measured in micrometers.

Why the spacing between visits mattered

Patients returned at different intervals. Two successive visits could be months apart for one patient and much farther apart for another. I used the actual time between visits so the model could allow measurements taken closer together to remain more alike after accounting for each patient’s trajectory.

How I chose the model

Compare the mean trends
I compared a flat mean, a straight line, a quadratic curve and a log-time curve by maximum likelihood. The quadratic had the lowest BIC. Its time terms in the final model were −1.634 × time + 0.221 × time², with time in years.
Compare how visits are related
With the same quadratic mean, I compared models by restricted maximum likelihood. Random intercepts and slopes let patients start at different thicknesses and change at different rates. Adding continuous-time AR(1) let the remaining within-patient correlation decay with time separation and gave the lowest BIC (4,078.6).

What age and baseline thickness added

Age

The coursework compared baseline thickness across ages and considered several ways age could relate to change over time. In the revised comparison, adding baseline age and its interaction with linear time did not improve BIC.

Age model comparison

Baseline thickness

In a separate model using follow-up visits, the group with higher starting GCC had an estimated 0.559 μm/year steeper decline. The groups were formed using the first measurement, so extreme first readings can move toward typical values at later visits even when the baseline visit is left out of the fit.

Exploratory subgroup model

Diagnostics and limits

I checked normalized residuals against fitted values and inspected a normal Q–Q plot. The comparisons are exploratory, and the coefficient intervals do not account for model selection. The curve describes the observed follow-up period, and the baseline-group comparison cannot establish that patients with higher starting thickness progress faster biologically.

The coursework explored individual profiles, alternative mean and covariance models, age and baseline severity. The September 2026 revision used a common mean for the covariance comparisons and updated the baseline-group analysis. Revision history.
